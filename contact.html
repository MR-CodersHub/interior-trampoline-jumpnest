Smooth Arrival

Three things to check before you leave home.

1. Sign Waiver Online Save 10 minutes at the front desk by having your waiver signed beforehand.
2. Bring or Buy Grip Socks Pack your Jumpnest grip socks or grab fresh pairs at reception for ₹80.
3. Arrive 15 Mins Before Slot Allows comfortable time for sock check, wristband collection, and lockers.
4. Dress for Movement Athletic clothing without buckles, snaps, dangling cords, or belts.
Visiting FAQ

Quick answers before your journey.

Do I need to book in advance or can we just walk in?

Walk-ins are welcomed, but booking online is strongly recommended for weekends, holidays, and evening sessions because we maintain strict court capacity caps for safety.

Is there an age limit for children?

JUMPNEST is designed for children aged 1 to 12. We have a dedicated gated Toddler Sanctuary (Little Loop) for 1–4 year-olds, while 6–12+ year-olds enjoy the high-flying trampoline courts and ninja track.

Do spectator parents need to purchase a ticket?

No, non-jumping parents have free entry to our cafe viewing lounge and seating areas! Only jumping participants require passes.
