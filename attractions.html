Pre-Flight Briefing

A two-minute parent safety checklist.

Follow these simple golden rules to ensure your family enjoys an injury-free, pure-fun experience.

Anti-Slip Grip Socks Mandatory Must be worn on all trampoline beds and soft play mats to prevent slips.
One Jumper Per Bed Never jump on the same mat as another jumper to prevent collision risks.
Sign Online Waiver in Advance Must be signed by parent/legal guardian once per calendar year.
Active Adult Supervision Parents must maintain active oversight of children under 6 in all zones.
