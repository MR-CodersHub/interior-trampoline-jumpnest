Got Questions?

Good to know before your visit.

Everything you need to know about socks, safety, waivers, and spectator rules.

🔍
Do I need to sign a waiver before jumping?

Yes, all jumpers must have a valid electronic waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian. You can complete it in 30 seconds online using our Express Waiver form to bypass the reception desk when you arrive!

Why are special grip socks required?

Approved anti-slip rubberized grip socks are mandatory on all trampoline and soft play surfaces. They ensure superior traction, preventing slips and protecting trampoline beds. You can buy them once for ₹80 and reuse them on every future visit.

Can parents accompany toddlers in the play zones?

Absolutely! Children aged 1–4 in our Little Loop and Cloud Club can be accompanied by 1 parent spectator free of charge to assist with climbing and balance.

What should children wear to jump?

Comfortable athletic wear like t-shirts, leggings, or sweatpants is ideal. Please avoid clothing with sharp zippers, metal buckles, dangling jewelry, or studded jeans.

Is there an entrance fee for parents who just want to watch?

No! Parents who are not jumping can enter and relax in our cafe observation lounge completely free. You get free Wi-Fi and a clear 360-degree panoramic view of all courts.

Can we bring our own birthday cake or outside snacks?

Outside food is permitted only with our booked Birthday Party packages (specifically birthday cakes and cupcakes). For everyday visitors, our on-site cafe offers organic juice boxes, pizzas, sandwiches, and barista coffee.
