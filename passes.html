Pass Questions

Pass details, plainly explained.

Can one pass be shared across multiple siblings?

Yes! Our 10-visit Family Loop pass allows you to redeem multiple visits in the same session for siblings or visiting cousins.

What happens if my pass is nearing its expiration date?

You can easily extend your pass by 30 days by recharging with any additional 5-visit pack before your expiry date.

Do pass holders need to reserve slots in advance?

Yes, reserving online is strongly recommended on weekends to guarantee entry when courts reach maximum safe capacity. Pass holders have priority slot selection!

Are grip socks included with the passes?

The 10-visit Family Loop pass includes 2 free pairs of reusable grip socks. Additional socks can be purchased for ₹80/pair.
