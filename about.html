About JUMPNEST

Designed like a club. Loved like a playground.

JUMPNEST was founded by parents who loved seeing their children bounce, climb, and laugh, but grew exhausted with noisy, dimly lit, cluttered play venues. We set out to create a bright, hygienic, beautifully designed indoor wonderland where kids can challenge their physical bodies and parents feel genuinely welcomed.

Our Philosophy

Children need freedom. Parents deserve calm.

We believe the very best indoor play spaces achieve both. That means high-flying trampoline courts, multi-tier soft play mazes, and sensory toddler circuits built to international safety benchmarks (EN 1176 / ASTM), combined with sound-absorbing architectural design and a relaxing cafe mezzanine.

Our digital experience mirrors our venue: fast online slot reservation, 30-second electronic waivers, transparent multi-visit pass tracking, and zero friction from arrival to the first bounce.

What Guides Us

Four commitments behind every corner.

01

Move with Purpose

Every zone stimulates a specific developmental skill: proprioception on trampolines, motor planning in the soft play maze, and balance on the ninja agility track.

02

Hospital-Grade Cleanliness

We perform daily UV-C fogging, sanitize ball pits every 48 hours with automated wash systems, and wipe down touchpoints between every single 90-minute session.

03

Acoustic Peace of Mind

Specialized acoustic ceiling panels reduce echoing and background decibel levels by 40%, keeping the space energizing without sensory fatigue for neurodivergent kids and parents.

04

Respect Family Time

No waiting in line to fill out paper waivers. You book online, check in with a quick scan, and head straight to the trampolines.
